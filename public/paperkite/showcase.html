FormatRising

Bean-to-cup process clips

Why it works. Calm, wordless shots of something being made keep people watching to the end, and finished views are what the feed rewards.

Why it fits. You roast every Thursday, so there is something new to film every week.

Rainy-day, cosy-season posts

2 · Why posts take off

Why a post takes off.

Every week the studio takes the best posts apart: what happens in the first second, what keeps people watching, when it went out. What worked goes into the next plan.

Nightjar Coffee Roasters' best post

“Roasted on Thursday”

Posted Monday 08:00

6Kviews

  1. 00–04sHookThe promise in the first second: “Roasted on Thursday.” over moving beans. No logo, no intro.
  2. 04–09sProcessBeans, bloom, espresso. Three quick shots of how it's made: proof instead of adjectives.
  3. 09–12sPayoff“In your cup by Friday.” turns the process into a reason to order.
  4. 12–21sHuman momentA hand, latte art, steam. Slow shots people watch to the end.
  5. 21–26sEnd cardName, street, what to do next. Nothing to read before this.
  6. TimingWent out Monday 08:00. The studio tries the same slot again before moving it.
What we repeat next week
  • Lead with the claim, not the logo.
  • Show how it's made, then the result.
  • Film one new process every roast day.

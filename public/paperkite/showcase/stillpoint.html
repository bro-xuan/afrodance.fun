Your first visit

Everything you need to know before you come.

  1. 01

    Arrive 10 minutes early

    We'll show you around, help you pick a spot and set up your props.

  2. 02

    Wear something comfy

    Clothes you can stretch in. Yoga is done barefoot, so no special shoes.

  3. 03

    Mats & props included

    Clean mats, blocks, straps and blankets are free. Bring your own if you prefer.

  4. 04

    Showers & lockers

    Two showers, towels for €1 and free lockers with a code lock.

  5. 05

    Tell us about injuries

    Let your teacher know before class; they'll offer alternatives for every pose.

  6. 06

    Step-free access

    Ground-floor studio with a step-free entrance and an accessible toilet.

Questions

I've never done yoga. Which class should I start with?+

Beginners' Flow or Breath & Restore. Both are slow, clearly explained and your first class is free.

Do I need to bring a mat?+

No. Mats, blocks, straps and blankets are included. You're welcome to bring your own.

How do I cancel a booking?+

Use the link in your confirmation email up to 6 hours before class and the credit goes straight back to your account.

Can I pause my membership?+

Yes, up to two months per year for travel, illness or pregnancy, free of charge.

Is there a student discount?+

Students and over-65s get 20% off all passes and memberships with a valid ID.

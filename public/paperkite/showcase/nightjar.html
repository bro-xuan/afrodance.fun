Our story

Most cafés buy coffee roasted weeks ago. We roast ours while the city sleeps, in batches of twelve kilos, so every cup you drink tomorrow morning was a green bean last night.

Good questions.

When do you deliver?+

Beans ordered by Tuesday 23:00 are roasted that week and delivered Thursday. Free from €30.

Can I pick up instead?+

Yes. Choose pick-up at checkout and collect at the café any day from 07:00.

Do you supply offices and restaurants?+

We do. Wholesale starts at 5 kg a month and includes grinder setup and barista training.

Are your farms certified?+

We buy direct and pay at least 2× the fair-trade minimum. Every bag lists the farm and the price we paid.

( 05 ) Good to know

FAQ

Do you take walk-ins?+

Yes, every day we're open. The live queue at the top of this page shows how many people are waiting. Booked appointments go first, so booking guarantees your time.

How do I cancel or move my booking?+

Use the link in your confirmation text or send us a WhatsApp. Free up to 4 hours before your appointment. Late cancellations or no-shows pay €10 next time.

How can I pay?+

Card, contactless, Apple Pay and Google Pay. We don't take cash. Tips are welcome but never expected.

Do you cut kids' hair?+

Yes, children under 12 pay €20. Saturday mornings get busy, so book ahead if you can.

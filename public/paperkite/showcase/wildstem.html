Good to know

Where do you deliver?+

Everywhere in postcodes RV1 to RV6, roughly 8 km around the shop. Delivery is €6.95, free on orders over €45. Further away? Call us and we'll try to help.

What's the cut-off for same-day delivery?+

Order before 14:00, Monday to Saturday, and we deliver between 15:00 and 19:00 the same day. We don't deliver on Sundays, but the shop is open 10:00–14:00 for pick-up.

How do I make my flowers last?+

Trim 2 cm off the stems at an angle, use a clean vase with fresh water and our flower food, and change the water every two days. Keep them away from direct sun, radiators and fruit bowls.

Can I pause or change my subscription?+

Yes. Skip a delivery, pause for the holidays or change the size anytime before 12:00 the day before. Just send us a WhatsApp or reply to your reminder email.

Do you do weddings and events?+

We do. Book a free 30-minute consultation and we'll send a full styling quote within 48 hours. We usually book weddings 3–9 months ahead.

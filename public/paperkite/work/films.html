Product commercial · Café & roastery

Nightjar Coffee Roasters “Roasted on Thursday”

0:269 shots16:9 + 9:16 “Dreamy Jazz”, licensed
0:00 / 0:00

A 25-second spot for a bean subscription. It follows the coffee from roast to cup, and the promise is in the title: roasted on Thursday, in your cup by Friday.

The brief

Sell the fortnightly bean subscription to people who already love the café, and make freshness feel like something you can see.

How we cut it

  • Macro shots of beans, bloom and crema, so the product fills the frame
  • Cuts timed to the music, with slow dissolves between pours
  • Two short lines of copy, then a clean end card with the address

Delivered

  • 25-second film for the website and YouTube
  • 9:16 cut for Reels, TikTok and Stories
  • Poster frames for the shop and newsletter

Interior & atmosphere film · Restaurant & bar

Brasserie Oost “Evenings at Oost”

0:2910 shots16:9 + 9:16 “Dim The Lights”, licensed
0:00 / 0:00

A half-minute walk through a restaurant on a busy night: pendant lights, a laid table, the open kitchen, a flambé at the table and the bar.

The brief

Show the room, not just the food. Guests book for the evening, so the film has to feel like walking in at seven.

How we cut it

  • Opens outside and moves inward: terrace, lights, dining room, table
  • Wide shots of the room alternated with close detail
  • Footage from different nights matched in the grade so it plays as one evening

Delivered

  • 29-second interior film for the website header
  • 9:16 reel for Instagram and Google Business Profile
  • Poster frames for booking platforms

Spa brand film · Spa, hammam & sauna

Lumen Bathhouse “Slow down. Warm up.”

0:2810 shots16:9 + 9:16 “Vastness”, licensed
0:00 / 0:00

A slow brand film for a spa: candlelight, a marble hammam, an open fire, warm oil massage and an infinity pool at dusk. Long dissolves, very little text.

The brief

Sell day passes and gift cards with a film that feels like the place itself: quiet and warm, nothing rushed.

How we cut it

  • Shots held for three seconds or more, with long cross-fades
  • Fire slowed to half speed to calm the pace
  • Only four words of copy before the end card

Delivered

  • 28-second brand film for the website and lobby screens
  • 9:16 cut for Reels and Stories
  • Poster frames for gift card pages

Treatment walkthrough · Skin clinic & wellness

Linen Skin Studio “The signature facial”

0:2810 shots16:9 + 9:16 “Serene View”, licensed
0:00 / 0:00

One therapist, one client, one treatment from start to finish. Five numbered steps show what actually happens in the sixty minutes.

The brief

New clients don't know what a facial involves. Show every step so booking feels safe, and keep it calm and clean.

How we cut it

  • The same therapist, client and room in every shot, so it plays as one session
  • Numbered step captions: Cleanse, Mask, Sculpt, Light therapy, Massage
  • A bright, soft edit to match the clinic's white interior

Delivered

  • 28-second walkthrough for the treatment page
  • 9:16 version for Instagram
  • Step stills for the treatment menu

Service film · Physio & massage

Fieldhouse “Stiff from the desk?”

0:238 shots16:9 + 9:16 “Valley Sunset”, licensed
0:00 / 0:00

A short film for a physio and sports-massage practice. It opens on the problem, then shows hands-on work on the neck, back, calves and feet.

The brief

Reach office workers nearby with a film that names the problem in the first second, then shows real treatment.

How we cut it

  • Opens with a question the viewer can answer
  • Close-ups of the therapist's hands make each technique clear
  • The end card gives appointment lengths and same-week availability

Delivered

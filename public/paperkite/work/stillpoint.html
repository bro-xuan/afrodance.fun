The brief

A small yoga studio with loyal regulars but empty beginner classes. New students couldn't find the timetable, didn't know what to book or bring, and asked the same questions on WhatsApp every week. They needed a site that turns curious visitors into booked first classes.

The idea

Put the two things a new student needs, “what's on” and “how do I start”, one tap from anywhere. The intro offer filters the timetable to beginner classes, every class books in under a minute, and everything a nervous first-timer wonders about is answered before they have to ask. Calm, breathing motion makes the site feel like the studio itself.

01

Book in under a minute

Filter the week by day and level, see spots left at a glance and book with a name and email. Full classes switch to a waitlist, and every booking ends with first-visit tips.

02

An offer that leads somewhere

“First class free” isn't just a banner: one tap filters the timetable to beginner-friendly classes, so new students go from curious to booked without hunting.

03

No surprises on price

Memberships, class packs and discounts sit side by side, and the monthly/yearly toggle animates the savings so choosing a plan feels obvious, not salesy.

04

Answers before questions

Teacher bios, a first-visit guide, reviews, an FAQ and a drawn map with hours and WhatsApp cut the repeat questions the studio used to answer by hand.

05

Calm, fast and findable

A soft WebGL aura and a one-minute breathing exercise bring the studio's mood online without slowing anything down, and structured data helps Google show hours, address and prices.

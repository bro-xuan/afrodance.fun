How it works

A strategist who understands your business, and an AI studio that does the work every week.

01

We learn your business

02

Talk to our team

Questions, answered.

Do I have to make the posts myself?

No. Every week your studio plans the posts from your photos and your facts. You look them over and say yes, in a few minutes, and you can change anything or write your own. On Full Studio our team prepares everything and you just approve from your phone.

Do I need a marketing person, or my own AI tools?

No. That is the point: the studio does what a marketing person would do. It knows your business, decides what to say this week and learns from what worked. You only need a phone.

How does the AI know my business?

From the kick-off: your offer, customers, tone of voice, photos and the facts you've confirmed. Every draft starts from that, plus a weekly look at what works for businesses like yours and at your own results.

Is everything made by AI?

AI does the heavy lifting: research, drafts, edits. You (or our team) decide what goes out. If an image is AI-made, the post says so.

Do you use stock photos?

Never for your products, your place or your people. Posts are made from your own photos, so customers see the real thing.

Do you need my passwords?

No. You connect your own accounts and can disconnect them any time.

Is there a long contract?

No. Plans are monthly and you can cancel any time. Prices are excluding VAT.

Is my data safe?

Your studio is private: you sign in with a personal link, and only you and our team can open it. Photos lose their location data when you upload them, your photos and facts are used only to make and publish your posts, and you can have your details deleted any time. We sign a data processing agreement on request.
